== 07 | runtime type information: objects that know their own type ==

One vtable, two jobs - dispatch (slot 1) and identity (slot 0):
  Stream                 buffered byte sink
  FileStream             fd=3
  CompressedFileStream   fd=4, ratio=75%
  Widget                 8x6 pixels

is_a two ways: a parent-chain walk and Cohen's O(1) display:
  CompressedFileStream is-a CompressedFileStream   walk=yes cohen=yes  (agree)
  CompressedFileStream is-a FileStream             walk=yes cohen=yes  (agree)
  CompressedFileStream is-a Stream                 walk=yes cohen=yes  (agree)
  CompressedFileStream is-a Widget                 walk=no  cohen=no   (agree)
  FileStream           is-a CompressedFileStream   walk=no  cohen=no   (agree)
  FileStream           is-a Stream                 walk=yes cohen=yes  (agree)
  Stream               is-a FileStream             walk=no  cohen=no   (agree)
  Widget               is-a Stream                 walk=no  cohen=no   (agree)
  walk is O(depth), cohen is O(1), and they never disagree

A checked downcast is a maybe you must branch on:
  a CompressedFileStream -> CompressedFileStream*: ok, ratio=75%
  a FileStream           -> CompressedFileStream*: NULL, took the safe branch

Filtering a heterogeneous array by type:
  keep is-a Stream:
    [0] Stream               kept
    [1] FileStream           kept
    [2] CompressedFileStream kept
    [3] Widget               dropped
    -> 3 of 4 matched
  keep is-a FileStream:
    [0] Stream               dropped
    [1] FileStream           kept
    [2] CompressedFileStream kept
    [3] Widget               dropped
    -> 2 of 4 matched
